1-Month Fingerstyle Coaching Package

Over one focused month, you’ll work directly with Nir to significantly elevate your playing — whether you’re refining technique, finding your voice, or finally mastering a song you’ve struggled with.

A Focused Masterclass Approach

You’ll bring 2–3 pieces you want to work on — ideally ones you’ve already begun exploring. These pieces become the core of our work. We’ll dissect, rebuild, and refine them as a way to explore:

  • Solving technical challenges with precision and ease
  • Shaping arrangements with clarity, intention, and tone
  • Bringing music to life through expressive phrasing and feel
  • Practicing with focus — not repetition — for real progress

The goal: to sharpen your technique, deepen your musicality, and stretch what you believe is possible — all through focused, intentional work.

By the End of the Month, You Will…

  • A clear, confident sound that reflects more of your style
  • A more refined technique that makes even tricky parts feel natural
  • One or more fingerstyle arrangement or song you can proudly perform
  • A deeper understanding of musical feel, tone, and dynamics
  • A practice routine that actually works for your level and life
  • New confidence that will take you forward in your guitar journey

Frequently Asked Questions

Can beginners apply?

Unfortunately not. These lessons are designed for intermediate and advanced guitarists focusing on fingerstyle technique. (There are so many great teachers for beginners!)

What do I need for online lessons?

A guitar, a stable internet connection, and a webcam - are all you need.

How long is each session?

Sessions are 55 minutes.

Do I have to commit to a full package?

Yes — all lessons are offered in 4-session packages. This structure helps ensure continuity and real progress.

How long do I have to complete the package?

All 4 lessons must be completed within 6 weeks of your first session to maintain learning momentum.
